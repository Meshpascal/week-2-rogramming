Importance of tolerance

Tolerance is an important value that helps people live together peacefully despite their differences. It means accepting and respecting other people's opinions, beliefs, cultures, and ways of life, even when they are different from our own. In today's world, tolerance promotes unity, understanding, and cooperation among individuals and communities. When we learn to tolerate one another, we reduce conflicts, build stronger relationships, and create a society where everyone feels respected and valued. Therefore, we should all practise tolerance in our daily lives because a peaceful society begins with mutual respect.
